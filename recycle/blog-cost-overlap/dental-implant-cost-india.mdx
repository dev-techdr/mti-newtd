A single dental implant fixture in India is listed at USD 500–900 in the 2026 TechDr guide. The crown is often quoted beside it, not inside it. Placement is usually a dental-chair visit. If a bone graft is needed, you may need two trips several months apart. The same guide lists USD 3,000–5,000 per tooth in the USA and USD 2,000–3,500 in the UK. The tooth you chew on is not always finished in one week.

</section>

## How much does one dental implant cost in India?

The guide figure is the fixture in the jaw. The abutment and the crown are separate parts of a finished tooth. A quote that prices only the screw is honest if it says so, and incomplete if it calls that price “a new tooth”. Several fixtures for a bridge or a denture multiply the plan. The bite design matters as much as the unit price.

## When is one trip not enough?

Active gum infection is treated first. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and untreated gum disease make an implant less likely to last. This page does not publish an unsourced survival percentage. A graft needs time to heal before the implant, or before the crown, depending on the dentist’s protocol. Ask which stages happen in India and which can be finished by a dentist at home. A five-day holiday cannot invent bone.

## What should the quote itemise?

Fixture, abutment, crown, graft if needed, the brand or an honest statement that the brand will be confirmed, and the number of visits in this trip. Sedation, if you want it and it is safe, is its own line. X-rays or a small scan of the jaw should be part of the plan before a fixture is promised. Send any recent dental X-rays and a list of medicines, especially blood thinners and diabetes drugs.

## What is the recovery like?

A soft diet for days. If the implant is buried, the final crown waits months. That wait can happen at home if a dentist there will fit the crown to the system that was placed. Ask for the implant details in writing so the dentist at home is not guessing the connection. Pain that worsens, swelling that spreads, or a fever needs same-day dental or medical care.

## How do you compare countries?

Compare a finished tooth with a finished tooth. An Indian fixture at USD 500–900 plus a named crown can still sit well below a USA or UK tooth that includes the crown. An Indian fixture compared with a home “complete implant crown” is not the same product. Add the flight, and add a second flight if the graft protocol needs one.
