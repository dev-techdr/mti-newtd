Bone marrow or stem-cell transplant in India is listed at USD 18,000–40,000 in the 2026 TechDr guide. Autologous transplants, using your own cells, tend toward the lower half. Donor transplants tend toward the top or above it. Hospital stay is often 3–6 weeks, with about 6–10 weeks in the city. The same guide lists USA figures from USD 300,000 and UK figures from USD 120,000. This is not a short admission.

</section>

## Why is the price a range and not one number?

An autologous transplant and an allogeneic transplant are different treatments. A donor transplant adds matching, a donor work-up, and the risk of graft-versus-host disease, where donor immune cells attack skin, gut, or liver. Haploidentical or cord-blood protocols usually sit at the high end. Unrelated-donor registry fees are extra unless the quote lists them. Relapse treatment is not included.

## What does the stay actually look like?

Conditioning chemotherapy comes first, then the cells are infused through a vein. Counts fall to near zero. That is expected, and it is why infection control matters more than the hotel. Visitors may be limited. Discharge to nearby housing is common before a flight is safe. Flying while neutropenic or febrile is unsafe. Clearance is a physician’s decision, often many weeks after counts recover.

## What should a quote separate?

Look for conditioning and the transplant admission, a day cap for supportive care, whether donor work-up is included, and how many days of housing after the ward are in the price. Months of housing, imported drugs, a second infusion, and ICU days beyond the cap move the bill. A caregiver who can stay the whole admission is part of the plan, not an optional extra.

## Which records does the physician need?

Pathology and genetic reports, staging scans, HLA typing if it exists, an infection screen, and a full treatment history. A transplant physician should review the slides and the stage before a date is discussed. Cure and remission rates vary by disease. A percentage copied from another diagnosis is misleading. Ask for figures that match your exact condition and stage.

## What continues after you fly home?

Immune recovery takes months. Crowds, some foods, and live vaccines are restricted on the unit’s instruction. A haematologist at home must continue prophylaxis. Fever after transplant is an emergency. Hair usually regrows. Fertility may not. Discuss sperm or egg storage before conditioning if a future pregnancy matters.

## How should you compare the cost with care at home?

Compare the pathway, not the infusion day. An Indian letter that names autologous versus donor, the registry fees, and the housing days is comparable with a home quote that names the same items. A headline of “transplant from USD 18,000” without the pathway is not a quote.
