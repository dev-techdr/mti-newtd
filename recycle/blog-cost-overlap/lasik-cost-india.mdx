LASIK in India is often planned around USD 800–1,500 for a straightforward pair of eyes in the 2026 TechDr ranges. It is a day procedure. Plan about 3–5 days in the city for the day-1 check. This is a planning range, not one of the locked comparison-table procedures. The same ranges list about USD 2,000–4,000 in the USA and USD 1,500–3,000 in the UK. Thin corneas and unstable prescriptions are reasons to say no.

</section>

## How much does LASIK cost in India?

The planning band assumes both eyes are suitable for a standard LASIK. PRK, or surface laser, is a different operation with a slower, sorer recovery and should be quoted on its own. An enhancement months later is not included. Keratoconus treatment is not included. If you are over the age where a cloudy lens is the real problem, a cataract-style lens procedure may be the better plan. Those prices are on the [cataract guide](/blog/cataract-surgery-cost-india), not here.

## Who should not buy a LASIK package?

People with thin corneas, a prescription that is still changing, keratoconus, or some retinal problems. A cheap package cannot override the scan. Very high glasses numbers have a limit to how much cornea can be removed. The surgeon may suggest a lens procedure instead. Wanting the operation is not itself an indication. TechDr will not sell LASIK to a scan that says no.

## What does the trip look like?

Remove contact lenses for the period the clinic sets before the scan. Do not have surgery the evening you land if the flight has dried your eyes. Both eyes are often treated in one sitting under anaesthetic drops. You feel pressure. Vision is misty, so you need an escort. Many people see well enough to walk the next day. Screens can still feel tiring. Do not book a same-night flight unless the surgeon has agreed in writing.

## What results are honest?

Many suitable candidates reach driving-standard vision without glasses in published series. Dry eye, night glare, and a small residual number still happen. Reading glasses in your 40s and 50s still happen. Rubbing the eyes in the first weeks is a real risk to the flap. Use the lubricants you are given. Sudden pain or falling vision needs urgent care.

## What should the quote say?

Both eyes or one, LASIK versus PRK, the pre-operative scan, the day-1 check, and the initial drops. Bring your last two glasses prescriptions so the team can see that the number has been stable. A quote that skips the scan and prices only the laser is incomplete.

## How do you use the price in a comparison?

Label it as a planning estimate. It is not in the same locked table as cataract, bypass, or joint replacement. Compare scan-included LASIK with scan-included LASIK. A home price for a lens exchange is a different operation.
