Cataract surgery in India is listed at USD 600–1,500 per eye in the 2026 TechDr guide for a standard monofocal lens. It is usually a day procedure or one night. Many people finish the operation and an early check within about a week. The same guide lists USD 3,500–6,000 per eye in the USA, USD 2,500–4,000 in the UK, and USD 1,500–3,000 in Thailand. Premium lenses cost more and do not suit every eye.

</section>

## How much does cataract surgery cost in India?

The band is one eye and a standard monofocal lens, which is sharpest at one distance. A toric lens for astigmatism, and a multifocal or extended-depth lens, are extras. They must be ordered to your measurements. Multifocal lenses can cause halos at night and are a poor match for significant retinal disease. The second eye is a second line unless the quote says both.

## Can both eyes be done on one trip?

Sometimes, with a gap of a few days. Some surgeons prefer to see the first result before touching the second eye. Ask which plan they use, because it changes the hotel nights. You need an escort on the day. Vision is blurred. The operation is done with numbing drops. Pressure is expected. Sharp pain should be reported.

## Will you still need glasses?

Often yes, for reading, if the monofocal lens is set for distance. Premium lenses reduce that need for some people. They are not a promise to abolish glasses. Surgery does not create a young eye. A scarred retina, glaucoma, or corneal disease limits the result even when the lens operation is technically smooth. If you have diabetes, the retina must be checked before anyone talks about a final prescription.

## What is excluded, including later laser?

Glaucoma treatment, retinal treatment, and a later YAG laser for a cloudy membrane are usually outside the package. YAG, if needed months later, is a short clinic procedure. Prostate drugs such as tamsulosin affect the iris during surgery. Put them on the medicine list. Bring your current glasses and any record of eye injury or previous laser.

## When can you fly?

Many surgeons allow a flight after the day-1 or week-1 review if the eye is calm. Follow the drop chart on the plane. Do not rub the eye and do not swim. Increasing pain or falling vision after you land needs same-day care at home.

## How should you compare quotes?

Compare the lens model, one eye or two, and whether the first review is included. An “imported lens” without a model name is not a specification. The measurement of the eye matters more than the country printed on the box. A home quote for a premium lens is not comparable with an Indian quote for a monofocal lens.
