Heart bypass surgery (CABG) in India is listed at USD 5,000–8,500 in the 2026 TechDr guide for a standard package. Many people stay 7–10 days in hospital and about 2–3 weeks in India. The same guide lists USD 70,000–150,000 in the USA, USD 25,000–40,000 in the UK, and USD 18,000–28,000 in Thailand. A heart attack in progress is an emergency at home, not a flight.

</section>

## How much does bypass surgery cost in India?

The band assumes a planned bypass, not an emergency transfer, not a valve replacement in the same operation, and not an open-ended intensive-care stay. The number that matters is the graft plan and the ICU cap on the hospital letter. TechDr asks a heart team to review the angiogram before any date is offered.

If the surgeon finds a vessel that cannot be grafted safely, the graft count can change in theatre. A material change should be explained. A promised “five grafts” on a brochure is not a surgical plan.

## What should the quote name?

Look for surgery and anaesthesia, whether the operation is expected on a heart-lung machine or on a beating heart, the ICU allowance in days, the ward nights, standard in-hospital medicines, and an English discharge summary. On-pump and off-pump are methods. The surgeon chooses from the vessels and from how stable you are. The method is not a discount tier.

A combined bypass and valve is a larger operation. It needs its own quote. Read the [valve guide](/blog/heart-valve-replacement-cost-india) if the echo also shows a leaking or tight valve.

## What moves the price?

Extra ICU days, a balloon pump, poor kidney function, a long diabetes history, and a return to theatre for bleeding are the usual movers. Cardiac rehabilitation at home, a later stent, and a defibrillator are not inside a standard bypass package. Dental infection should be treated before a planned bypass, because infected teeth are a risk around heart surgery.

## How long before a flight is reasonable?

Uncomplicated patients are often discussed for travel around two to three weeks after surgery, and only with written clearance. The breastbone is still healing. You should not lift a cabin bag. Fever, breathlessness, an unstable wound, or a new rhythm problem means you do not board.

Bring the angiogram images, not only the printed report. Most surgeons want a catheter angiogram before bypass. A CT report alone is often not enough. Do not stop aspirin or other heart drugs unless the cardiologist writes the date.

## How does the price compare with treatment at home?

Using the guide ranges, a planned bypass in India sits far below private USA and UK figures, and below the Thailand band in the same table. The honest comparison adds flights for you and a companion, a hotel after discharge, and time off work. A cancelled flight home because of a wound or a rhythm problem is part of the real cost.

## What should you ask before you pay?

Ask who is in the operating room in terms of seniority, how many ICU days are included, the price of one extra ICU day, and who you pay. Pay a hospital invoice. Do not pay a stranger’s personal account to “hold a cardiac bed”. A deposit, if one is asked, should be written and refundable if the surgeon later says you are not fit to travel.

## Sources and how to use this guide

The USD ranges are the 2026 TechDr indicative guide, not a hospital tariff and not a survival promise. Ask the centre for audited outcomes if you want local figures. Survival depends on heart function, age, urgency, and whether other arteries or valves are involved.
