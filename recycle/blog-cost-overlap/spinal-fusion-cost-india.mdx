A straightforward one-level spinal fusion in India is listed at USD 6,000–9,500 in the 2026 TechDr guide, including theatre, standard implants, and the hospital stay. Many people stay 4–7 days in hospital and about 2–3 weeks in India. The same guide lists USD 80,000–110,000 in the USA and USD 25,000–35,000 in the UK. More levels cost more. Many slipped discs do not need fusion at all.

</section>

## How much does spinal fusion cost in India?

Assume one level until the quote counts screws, rods, and cages. Two levels, a revision of old metal, and a cervical fusion are different operations. A lumbar price does not apply to the neck. Minimally invasive fusion is not automatically cheaper. Smaller incisions can carry a similar implant cost. The technique should match the anatomy.

## When is fusion the wrong offer?

Ordinary muscle pain and many disc herniations improve without screws. Some need only a decompression. Fusion is for instability, selected spondylolisthesis, some fractures, and some deformity. TechDr would rather return a file with “no operation” than book metal you do not need. Send the MRI files and standing X-rays. A report without images is rarely enough.

## What should the quote list?

The number of levels, whether a cage is included, lumbar versus cervical, decompression if a nerve is pinched, ward nights, and the price of an extra night. Navigation technology, a custom implant, a stimulator, and a long-term brace are extras unless listed. Smoking makes fusion healing poorer. Say if you smoke, and say if you take osteoporosis medicines or blood thinners.

## How long before you can fly?

After an uncomplicated lumbar fusion, surgeons often discuss flying at about two to three weeks, sometimes longer. Sitting still and wound healing matter more than cabin pressure. New weakness, fever, or loss of bladder control is an emergency, not a travel delay to negotiate. Spine implants can trigger airport metal detectors. Carry the implant card.

## What results are reasonable to expect?

Carefully chosen patients often report less leg pain. Back pain relief is less predictable. Bone may fail to fuse, and a neighbouring level can wear over the years. Published series are not a promise for your scan. Desk work is often discussed at 4–8 weeks if the surgeon agrees. Heavy lifting waits.

## How do you compare a fusion quote with care at home?

Count the implants on both letters. A home quote that prices the surgeon and leaves the cage as a separate invoice is not comparable with an Indian package that includes the cage, or the reverse. Add flights, a companion, and a hotel for the wound check. The [package guide](/blog/whats-included-in-a-medical-tourism-package) shows the lines that should be present on either letter.
