Angioplasty with one stent in India is listed at USD 3,500–5,500 in the 2026 TechDr guide. Hospital stay is often 1–3 days, with about 7–10 days in India if the puncture site and symptoms are stable. The same guide lists USD 30,000–50,000 in the USA and USD 12,000–18,000 in the UK. A second or third stent is not a free extra. New chest pain this week needs a local emergency department.

</section>

## How much does one stent cost in India?

The band is for one stent in a planned procedure. More stents, a complicated artery, an unplanned switch to bypass, and a long stay for a kidney problem sit outside it. Ask for the stent category in writing. A drug-eluting stent is the usual default. An unnamed “imported stent” is not a specification. Bioresorbable stents are not the default and should be priced only if the cardiologist recommends one.

## When is a stent the wrong package?

Left-main disease, extensive multi-vessel disease, and some people with diabetes are often safer with bypass. That decision comes from the angiogram and a heart team, not from which package is cheaper. If your films already suggest surgery, read the [bypass cost guide](/blog/heart-bypass-cost-india-2026) before you accept a one-stent headline.

## What must the quote include besides the stent?

The procedure, the named stent count, the bed nights, and the antiplatelet medicines you must not stop. Stopping those tablets early is a recognised cause of stent clotting. If the quote covers only the day of the procedure, budget the full course your cardiologist names, or ask for that course to be written in.

Contrast dye can stress kidneys. Send the latest creatinine. The team may give fluids, limit dye, or delay the procedure. That is a safety step, not a hidden fee to argue with after you land.

## How soon can you fly?

Many stable patients fly after several days, once a clinician has cleared them. Plan 7–10 days in India unless your cardiologist wants longer. Chest pain, a swelling or bleed at the wrist or groin, or breathlessness means you should not board. You are usually sedated, not under a full general anaesthetic. Pressure is expected. Sharp pain is not.

## What should you send?

Send the angiogram images if you already have them, the echocardiogram, an ECG, a medicine list, and kidney tests. If you have no angiogram yet, say so. Some people need that test in India before anyone can honestly choose a stent or a bypass. Do not stop heart medicines to “prepare for travel” unless a cardiologist writes the instruction.

## How do you compare the price with care at home?

Put the Indian letter beside a home quote that also counts the stent, the lab, and the night. A surgeon-fee-only number at home is not the same product. After flights and a hotel, many planned one-stent trips still cost less than a private bill in the USA or UK. The saving disappears if you fly during unstable pain and need emergency care on arrival.
