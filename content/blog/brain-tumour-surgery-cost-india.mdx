Brain tumour surgery in India is listed at USD 6,500–10,000 in the 2026 TechDr guide for a standard craniotomy. ICU and ward time are often 5–10 days, with about 2–3 weeks in India if recovery is smooth. The same guide lists USD 75,000–150,000 in the USA and USD 30,000–50,000 in the UK. Radiotherapy and drugs are not inside the surgical price. You should not travel alone.

</section>

## How much does brain tumour surgery cost in India?

The band is a straightforward craniotomy, not awake mapping near speech or movement, not a long ICU stay, and not a shunt or a second operation for bleeding. A biopsy, when removal would be unsafe, is a different procedure and should be priced as one. Radiosurgery is not an open operation and should not share a headline with a craniotomy. Pathology after surgery guides the next treatment. That next treatment is a separate plan, often partly at home.

## What decides risk more than the city?

Location. A surface meningioma and a tumour in speech cortex or the brainstem are different operations. The aim is a safe removal or a biopsy, not a promise that every cell is gone. Ask which function the surgeon is protecting, and ask for that sentence in the letter. If you are deteriorating quickly, the nearest neurosurgical unit is the right place. A commercial flight is the wrong place for a falling conscious level.

## What should you send?

The MRI files, not a photo of a film, any biopsy, a seizure diary, and the exact doses of steroids and anti-seizure drugs. A companion must be able to consent if you are drowsy. Do not change steroid doses for the flight unless the neurosurgeon writes the instruction.

## What does recovery and the flight home look like?

Some people walk within days. Speech, vision, or strength can take months, and some deficits persist. Flying is discussed around two to three weeks after a smooth craniotomy, and only with a fitness letter. The cabin plan includes a companion and the exact medicines. Fever, a worsening headache, new weakness, or a seizure that is not your usual pattern needs the operating hospital, or emergency care if you have already landed.

## What is excluded?

Radiotherapy, chemotherapy, radiosurgery, long rehabilitation, and a return to theatre beyond the package terms. Histopathology and the scans the package lists should be marked in or out. An English operation note and the pathology report, when it is ready, belong in your cabin bag. Your neurologist or oncologist at home cannot continue a plan from a verbal summary.
