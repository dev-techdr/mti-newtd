<section id="quick-answer" aria-label="Quick answer">

Choose a hospital in India by checking NABH or JCI on the accreditor’s own directory, then by reading the written plan for your operation. NABH is India’s national hospital accreditation. JCI is an international one. A logo on a brochure is not the check. Accreditation covers systems such as infection control, consent, and medication safety. It does not predict your result, and it does not replace the implant name, the ICU cap, or the exclusions.

</section>

## What do NABH and JCI actually certify?

They review how a hospital runs: identification of patients, infection control, consent, records, and how emergencies are handled inside that building. They do not certify a surgeon’s hands for your diagnosis, and they do not set a price. Two accredited hospitals can both be reasonable. The better one for you is the one whose letter matches your scans.

Seniority of the person who will operate should still be described in role terms: who is in the room, and whether a trainee will operate under supervision. You can accept that or ask for the senior. You cannot choose if nobody tells you.

## How do you verify a logo?

Search the hospital’s legal name on the NABH and JCI public directories. Match the campus, not a group brand. A flagship may be accredited while a newer wing or a different city is not. If the directory listing has expired, treat the logo as unfinished business and ask for the current certificate before you pay.

No accreditation is not automatically a reason to refuse a clinic for a minor procedure, and it is a reason to slow down for major surgery. A tour of a lobby is not a substitute for the directory check.

## What should you ask after the logo checks out?

Use the ten questions in the [hospital choice guide](/blog/how-to-choose-a-hospital-abroad). The ones that change bills are: what is included, what an extra ICU day costs, who pays if you return to theatre, and who sees you after you fly home. Accreditation does not answer those. A safety page without a quote is incomplete, and a cheap quote from a hospital you cannot verify is also incomplete.

## Does accreditation change the medical visa or the city?

No. The [e-Medical Visa guide](/blog/india-e-medical-visa-guide) is a separate process. Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Kolkata can all host accredited care. The city should follow the team that accepted your file, a sensible flight, and a hotel close enough for wound checks. The [city guide](/blog/best-cities-india-medical-treatment) is about that match, not a ranking of skylines.

## What accreditation cannot do?

It cannot make an unfit flight safe. It cannot approve a paid organ donor. It cannot turn a surgery package into a year of cancer care. It cannot replace a fitness-to-fly letter. If a coordinator answers a clinical question with “we are JCI” and will not put the ICU days in writing, you still do not have a plan.
