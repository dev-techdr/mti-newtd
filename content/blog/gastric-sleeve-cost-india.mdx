Sleeve gastrectomy in India is listed at USD 5,000–7,000 in the 2026 TechDr guide for the operation, a short hospital stay, and standard in-hospital care. Most people stay 2–4 days in hospital and about 10–14 days in India. A gastric bypass is a different operation, listed at USD 6,500–9,500. Weight loss is not guaranteed. The same sleeve guide lists USD 20,000–30,000 in the USA and USD 10,000–15,000 in the UK.

</section>

## How much does a gastric sleeve cost in India?

The band is a primary laparoscopic sleeve. A revision of an old operation, a bypass, a gastric balloon, a long ICU stay, and later skin-removal surgery are not inside it. If you have a significant hiatus hernia or severe reflux, the surgeon may add a repair or advise a bypass instead. That changes the quote. Do not hide heartburn to fit a cheaper package.

## Who is a candidate to travel for a sleeve?

This is gastrointestinal surgery, not cosmetic surgery for a few kilos. Teams look at body mass index, type 2 diabetes, sleep apnoea, joint disease, and whether supervised weight loss has already been tried. TechDr does not book a date until a bariatric team has reviewed the history and said you are a candidate. Many teams will not operate soon after smoking. Say if you use a CPAP machine, and send a sleep study if you have one.

## What happens after the operation, and what is excluded?

You walk early and start a liquid diet, then purees, on a dietitian’s sheet. Flying is discussed only when you are drinking enough. Lifelong vitamins, protein targets, and blood tests for iron, B12, and vitamin D are your plan at home. A year of shakes is usually not in the package. Diabetes doses can change quickly. Only the physician managing your diabetes should change them. Bring a surplus of your current medicines.

Published series often describe substantial excess-weight loss over 12–18 months when follow-up continues. Some people regain weight. Leak, bleeding, and clot are the early serious risks. Reflux and vitamin gaps can appear later.

## Sleeve or bypass?

A bypass is quoted separately at about USD 6,500–9,500 in the TechDr planning ranges. It is not a deluxe sleeve. Severe reflux, some previous stomach operations, and the team’s reading of your history decide which operation is safer. Ask for the operation name on the letter, not “weight-loss surgery”.

## What should you send?

Recent weight and height, HbA1c, a medicine list, an abdominal ultrasound if you know you have gallstones, and any previous bariatric notes. Gallbladder disease found on arrival is often excluded unless it was quoted. Plan who will shop and cook the liquid diet when you get home.

## How do you judge the quote?

Look for the operation name, the dietitian sessions, leak tests the hospital actually uses, ward nights, and an exclusion list that mentions vitamins and later plastic surgery. A single round number with no list is not a package. Pay the hospital invoice.
