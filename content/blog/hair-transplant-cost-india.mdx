<section id="quick-answer" aria-label="Quick answer">

A hair transplant session in India is listed at USD 1,000–2,500 in the 2026 TechDr guide. It is usually a day procedure under local anaesthetic, with about 5–7 days in the city so the first wash can be taught. The same guide lists USD 8,000–15,000 in the USA and USD 5,000–10,000 in the UK. Density is not promised. A second session is common for a large area. Very large graft counts can cost more than the band.

</section>

## How much does a hair transplant cost in India?

Ask for the price per graft and the maximum the team will take from your donor area. A “full head” price sold before anyone sees your scalp is marketing. FUE punches out individual grafts and leaves tiny dot scars. FUT removes a strip and leaves a linear scar, which matters if you wear very short hair. Beard or body hair is a rescue option with a different texture and should be quoted separately.

## What decides the graft count?

The donor area at the back and sides. Transplanted hair is moved, not created. If the donor zone is thin, no technique invents coverage. Send unedited daylight photos of the hairline, the crown, and the donor area, plus any previous transplant photos. A low, dense teenage hairline copied from a filtered photo wastes grafts and looks wrong. Surgeons should refuse that design.

## What does the week look like, and when does hair show?

You sleep with the head raised. Crusts form over 7–14 days. Transplanted shafts often shed in the following weeks. That shed is expected and is not the result. Growth is judged at 6–12 months. Shock loss of existing hair can happen. Smoking, a weak donor area, and an unrealistic density request change how much transplanted hair grows. This page does not publish an unsourced survival percentage.

## What is excluded?

A second session, PRP or medicines unless listed, and repair of a donor area that was over-harvested elsewhere. Medicines such as finasteride or minoxidil are a medical decision. Transplanted hair does not stop later loss of the hair you still have. Forehead swelling, infection, and numbness that lasts months are known issues. Increasing redness, pus, or sudden patchy loss should be sent to the team the same day.

## How do you compare a quote?

Compare graft bands, the technique, and whether a maximum harvest is written down. A session at the bottom of the Indian range and a session at the top are not the same number of grafts. USA and UK bands in the guide are also per session, not a lifetime of hair. Add the flight. The operation itself is a short trip, which is why a one-week plan is realistic and a same-night departure is not.
