## How is an agency different from a hospital?

A hospital admits you, operates, and writes the discharge note. An agency coordinates. Mixing the two is how people pay the wrong company. If a website says it is both the surgeon and the travel desk, ask which legal name is on the invoice and which name is on the consent form. They should not be a private individual in another country.

TechDr is not a hospital. The [about page](/about) states the legal entity, the address in Secunderabad, the GSTIN, and the contact emails and WhatsApp numbers. Coordinators can work in English, Arabic, French, Russian, Bengali, and Hindi. Language help is part of an agency’s job. It is not a substitute for the clinical letter.

## What should the agency send you before you pay?

A written plan that names the operation and the exclusions. An itemised estimate in USD, labelled as indicative until the hospital letter replaces it. The 2026 ranges on this site are starting estimates, not a personal quote. A statement of what a deposit covers, and whether it comes back if the visa is refused or the surgeon says you are not fit. The party you pay. TechDr does not ask you to pay a stranger’s personal account.

After you accept, the partner hospital can issue an invitation letter. You still apply for the visa yourself. The agency does not stamp a passport. Read the [visa guide](/blog/india-e-medical-visa-guide) before you buy tickets.

## What does the agency handle on the ground?

Airport pickup against the flight number you send. A hotel booked near the campus in the quote, not across the city. A companion’s practical questions. English papers for the doctor at home, listed in the [records guide](/blog/records-to-carry-home-after-surgery). Video follow-up after you land is coordination. It is not an emergency department. Chest pain, a fever after transplant, or a draining wound needs local emergency care.

## What should an agency refuse?

Paid organ donation. A promised cure or a promised number of kilos, grafts, or years of implant survival. A flight for someone who is unstable. Surrogacy arrangements. A case that depends on disguising a donor’s relationship. An agency that treats those as add-on packages is optimizing for the booking, not for you. The [facilitator guide](/blog/best-medical-tourism-facilitators-india) uses the same refusals. Facilitator and agency are the words patients use. The job is the same if the letter is the same.

## How do you compare agencies without a public ranking?

There is no government ranking of a medical tourism agency in India. Ask each agency the same five questions, in one email. Who is the legal entity? Which hospital invoice will I pay? What is excluded? What happens to the deposit if the visa is refused? Who answers a wound photo after I fly home? Keep the answers. A phone promise that never becomes a paragraph is not a comparison.

Ask who pays the agency, you or the hospital, and whether that changes your price. Get that sentence in writing. Do not assume a “free” agency has no commercial relationship with the hospital. Ask, and then judge the hospital letter on its own.
