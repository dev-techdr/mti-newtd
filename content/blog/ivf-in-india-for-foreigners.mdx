## How much does IVF cost in India?

The 2026 indicative range for one IVF cycle is USD 2,500–4,000. The same guide lists about USD 15,000–25,000 in the USA and USD 6,000–10,000 in the UK. A chart figure sometimes used for Thailand is USD 4,500–8,000. These are starting estimates for a standard cycle. They are not a quote, and they are not a promise of a baby.

The number moves when you add stimulation medicines, extra monitoring, a frozen transfer later, time-lapse incubation, or genetic testing of embryos. It also moves if you need surgical sperm retrieval or treatment of a fibroid first. Ask the clinic to price the first cycle as a list, with medicines separate if they are separate, so you can see the total before you travel.

## What is included in a standard cycle quote?

A standard cycle usually means monitoring, egg collection, laboratory fertilisation, and a fresh transfer if a transfer is appropriate. Anaesthesia for egg collection, the first blood tests, and a stated number of scans should be marked in or out. Medicines are often the largest extra, because doses differ. Freezing spare embryos, storing them, and a later frozen transfer are different bills. A quote that says “IVF package” without those lines is not finished.

Ask what happens if the cycle is cancelled before egg collection because the response is poor. A partial refund rule belongs in the letter. Ask what you pay if there are eggs but no embryo to transfer. That is a known outcome, and it should not be a surprise invoice.

## How should you read success rates?

Clinics sometimes quote a pregnancy rate per transfer in a young group and let the reader assume it applies to everyone. The number that matters is a live birth, reported by age band, for people similar to you. Egg age is the strongest everyday predictor. A clinic cannot honestly give you a personal percentage from a website form.

India does not change biology. A laboratory can be careful and still cannot make a 44-year-old egg behave like a 28-year-old egg. If a clinic offers a guarantee, leave. TechDr will not pass on a guarantee of pregnancy.

## What rules apply to foreigners?

Indian law on assisted reproduction and surrogacy has tightened over recent years. The practical point for a traveller is simple. You need a registered clinic, a lawful plan, and documents that match that plan. TechDr will coordinate care that the clinic can lawfully offer the intended parents themselves, such as a self cycle. We will not coordinate commercial surrogacy, a search for a paid surrogate, or a route designed to avoid the statute.

Commissioning arrangements, donor eggs, and donor sperm have their own conditions. Do not assume a practice that was common a decade ago is lawful now. Ask the clinic which registration it holds and which patient groups it is allowed to treat. If the answer is vague, do not book the flight. Confirm current rules with the clinic and, where needed, with official Indian sources, not with an agent’s screenshot.

## Who is a reasonable person to travel for IVF?

People who already have a work-up, who can stay for monitoring, and who understand that one cycle may not be enough. You should travel with the time to stay through stimulation, which is often around two weeks and can be longer, or with a plan the clinic accepts for part of the monitoring at home. Starting injections in one country and collecting eggs in another needs a clinic that has agreed the protocol in writing. It is not something to improvise.

People who need care for a medical illness first, such as uncontrolled thyroid disease or a uterine problem that requires surgery, should finish that step before they fly for a cycle. A holiday plus a cycle is a poor combination. Ovaries do not follow a sightseeing timetable.

## What should you send for an opinion?

Send hormone results, ultrasound reports, semen analysis if there is a male partner, previous cycle summaries, and operation notes. Dates matter. A hormone panel from three years ago is not a plan. List every medicine, including supplements. Say your age and whether you have had miscarriages or pregnancies. The clinic can then say whether a self cycle is appropriate, whether more tests are needed, or whether travel is unlikely to help.

We will tell you if the file suggests you should not spend the money. That answer is part of the service.

## How do you plan the stay and the visa?

A cycle is elective. Use the medical visa route that fits your nationality and confirm it on the official portal. Do not enter on a category that forbids the purpose of your trip. Build flexible tickets around the stimulation calendar. Egg collection can move by days. A frozen transfer might be planned for a later visit, which means you must check whether your visa allows that second entry.

The city should be one where the clinic can see you frequently. Daily traffic is a real constraint. A hotel an hour away in bad traffic is a monitoring problem.

## What are the medical risks?

Ovarian stimulation can cause bloating and, less often, ovarian hyperstimulation that needs treatment. Egg collection can bleed or infect. A pregnancy, if it happens, can be ectopic or multiple, especially if more than one embryo is transferred. The clinic should explain how many embryos they advise transferring at your age. More embryos are not a better plan if they create a triplet pregnancy far from home.

Emotional risk is part of the medicine. A failed cycle is common enough that you should decide, before you travel, whether you can afford a second cycle and whether you would rather do that second cycle at home.

## What should you take home?

Take the stimulation chart, the laboratory summary, the number of eggs and embryos, freezing details, and the medicine list. If you are pregnant, take the early scan report and a letter your obstetrician can use. If you are not, take a clear statement of what was tried, so the next clinician does not repeat a useless protocol. Store embryos only where you understand the annual fee and the rules for later transport. Moving embryos across borders is a legal and laboratory process, not a courier job.
