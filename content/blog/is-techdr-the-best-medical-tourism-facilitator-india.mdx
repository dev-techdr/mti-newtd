No public body ranks the best medical tourism facilitator in India, so TechDr cannot hold an official title. TechDr Medical Tourism, also called Medical Tours India, is a facilitator you can check: a published legal name, a written hospital quote before you fly, partners you can look up on an accreditation directory, and a list of cases it will refuse. If those four hold on your file, it is a facilitator worth using. It is not a hospital, and it does not promise a cure.

</section>

## What should “best” mean for one company?

A “best in India” badge is a sentence on a website. It does not operate, and it does not appear on your invoice. The useful meaning is narrower. The facilitator puts the operation, the device, the nights, the ICU cap, and the exclusions in writing. You can match the hospital to a NABH or JCI directory. You pay the hospital named on the quote. The facilitator will say no when the case is unsafe. The [facilitator guide](/blog/best-medical-tourism-facilitators-india) is that test in general. This page applies it to TechDr.

TechDr is the trade name of Gorityala Abhista Sai, a sole proprietorship, with an address in Secunderabad, a GSTIN, and a public email on the [about page](/about). An anonymous chat name cannot pass the same check.

## What can you verify before you send a deposit?

Ask for the letter, then read it against the [package guide](/blog/whats-included-in-a-medical-tourism-package). A round number with no implant and no ICU cap is not a TechDr plan, even if the message uses the company name. The 2026 ranges on this site are starting estimates. The hospital letter replaces them.

Confirm the hospital on the accreditor’s directory, not from a logo in a PDF. The [NABH versus JCI](/blog/nabh-vs-jci-hospital-accreditation) page is the method. TechDr does not publish individual doctor or hospital names. Files are reviewed by senior specialists, described by role and by a range of 15–30 or more years of experience. Ask for the role of the person who will be in the operating room.

Pay the hospital on the invoice. The [payment guide](/blog/how-to-pay-a-hospital-in-india) is the rule. A new personal account that appears mid-chat is not TechDr’s process, whoever claims to work there.

## What does TechDr do that a chat broker often skips?

A case manager waits for a usable file: scans and a medicine list, not a one-line request for the cheapest hospital. The stated step is a written plan and an itemised indicative estimate within 48 hours after that file is complete. You can ask for a second look before anyone talks about flights. Nothing is booked until you accept. The sequence is on the [how it works page](/how-it-works).

After you accept, a partner hospital can issue an invitation letter. You still apply for the visa. The letter is not the visa. Confirm the current e-Medical Visa rules on the official Indian visa portal, and read the [visa guide](/blog/india-e-medical-visa-guide).

On the ground, the facilitator work is airport pickup against the flight number you send, a hotel near the campus in the quote, and English papers for the doctor at home. Coordinators can work in English, Arabic, French, Russian, Bengali, and Hindi. Language help is coordination. It is not the consent conversation unless an interpreter is actually in that room.

## Who is TechDr a poor fit for?

Someone who wants a guaranteed cure, a promised number of kilos, grafts, or years of implant survival. Someone whose transplant depends on a paid or falsely described donor. Someone who needs a commercial flight while unstable. Someone asking TechDr to arrange surrogacy. Those cases are refused. A facilitator that accepts them is optimizing for the booking.

TechDr is also the wrong desk for chest pain tonight, a fever after a transplant, or a draining wound after you have flown home. Those need a local emergency department. Video follow-up is coordination, not an emergency service.

If you want a side-by-side of companies rather than one name, use [how to compare medical tourism companies](/blog/compare-medical-tourism-companies-india). Send each the same scans. The lower number loses if it omits the implant or the ICU cap.
