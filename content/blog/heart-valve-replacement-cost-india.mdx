Heart valve replacement in India is listed at USD 7,500–11,000 in the 2026 TechDr guide for a standard surgical valve. Hospital stay is often 7–12 days, with about 3 weeks in India. The same guide lists USD 80,000–170,000 in the USA and USD 30,000–45,000 in the UK. Repair, when the leaflet can be saved, is a different operation and a different quote.

</section>

## How much does valve surgery cost in India?

The band is for a planned replacement of one valve with a standard prosthesis. A second valve, grafts for blocked arteries at the same time, a long ICU stay, and a switch from a hoped-for repair to a replacement can move the bill. The echo, not a package name, decides whether repair is realistic. Repair is often planned around USD 7,000–12,000 in the TechDr planning ranges, and only when the surgeon believes the leaflet can be saved.

## Mechanical or tissue: what changes the quote?

A mechanical valve usually means warfarin for life and regular blood tests. A tissue valve may avoid lifelong warfarin and is more likely to wear out over the years. Neither is “the premium option” for every age. The quote should name which valve is expected, and the discharge letter must say which blood-thinner plan applies to you. A successful repair often does not need lifelong warfarin. Do not assume that sentence applies if the operation became a replacement.

## What should the letter include?

Surgery, anaesthesia, the valve type, ICU days, ward nights, and in-hospital medicines. Cardiac rehabilitation at home is separate. A companion should travel with you, because the first days are in intensive care. Do not stop blood thinners on your own before the flight. The heart team writes the stop date.

## How long do you stay, and who should not fly yet?

Plan about three weeks in India for an uncomplicated replacement so the wound, the rhythm, and the first echo can be checked. Fever, breathlessness, a draining wound, or a new weakness that could be a stroke means you stay. If you are in active heart failure or have chest pain at rest, the nearest capable hospital is the right place. A commercial flight is not a cardiac ICU.

## What records speed up the opinion?

Send the echo images, not only the report, a coronary angiogram if one was done, and the medicine list. The surgeon may still want an angiogram in India before opening the chest. Say if you have had a previous heart operation. A redo sternotomy is not a primary valve price.

## How do you compare countries?

The guide ranges put a standard surgical valve in India well below the USA and UK bands before you add flights. Compare like with like: valve type, ICU cap, and whether bypass grafts are included. An Indian “package” against a home “surgeon fee” is not a comparison.
