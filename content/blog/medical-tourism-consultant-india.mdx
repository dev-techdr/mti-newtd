A medical tourism consultant in India can explain your options, collect a hospital quote, and organise the trip. They cannot decide that you are fit to fly, operate on you, or issue a visa. At TechDr, a case manager checks the file and senior specialists review it. Those reviewers are described by role and experience, not by a public name. The decision to travel stays yours, after you have the letter.

## What is a consultant in this setting?

Patients use “consultant” for two different people. One is the coordinator you message. The other is the specialist who reads the scans. Both can be called a consultant. Only the second one can form a clinical view, and even that view is an opinion, not a booking. TechDr’s about page says files are reviewed by senior consultants with about 15–30 or more years of experience. The website does not publish their names or the hospital names. Ask for the role of the person who will be in the operating room. A famous name on a poster is not that answer.

The coordinator’s job is to make the file complete, chase the missing scan, and put the quote in writing. A good coordinator says when the file is not ready. A photo of a screen can start the conversation. It is not a final plan. The [document guide](/blog/documents-for-a-treatment-quote) lists what to send.

## What can they decide, and what can they not?

They can tell you that a partner hospital has accepted the case, what the indicative price includes, and how long teams usually ask you to stay. They can request an invitation letter after you accept. They can book pickup and a hotel.

They cannot guarantee a bed on a date the hospital has not confirmed. They cannot overrule a transplant committee. They cannot tell you to stop blood thinners, diabetes drugs, or antiplatelet tablets. Those instructions come from the treating team, in writing. They cannot manage chest pain tonight. That is a local emergency department.

A remote opinion can stop a wasted flight. Read [how a second opinion works](/blog/free-second-opinion-online). It does not replace the doctor who will see you at home after surgery.

## Which questions separate a useful consultant from a salesperson?

Ask what the quote excludes, what an extra ICU day costs, and who you pay. Ask what the consultant will do if the surgeon later says you should not travel. Ask which language the discharge summary will use. Ask who replies after you land. If every answer is “we are the best medical tourism company”, you have not been given a plan. The [agency guide](/blog/best-medical-tourism-agency-india) and the [facilitator guide](/blog/best-medical-tourism-facilitators-india) are the same test, written for the words people type into search.

## How fast should a first view come back?

TechDr’s stated step is a written plan and an itemised indicative estimate within 48 hours after a case manager has a usable file. The clock starts when the scans and the medicine list are actually there, not when a one-line “need best hospital” message arrives. You can ask for a second look before anyone talks about flights. No hospital is booked before you accept.

## What should you keep from the conversation?

The written quote, the name of the legal entity, and the invoice party. Save them outside the chat app. A voice note is a weak record if a deposit is disputed later. Pay from the [payment guide](/blog/how-to-pay-a-hospital-in-india), not from a new number that appears mid-chat.
