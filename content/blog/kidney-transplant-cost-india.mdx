Kidney transplant packages in India are listed at USD 13,000–18,000 in the 2026 TechDr guide for the recipient surgery plan. Hospital stay is often 7–14 days for the recipient. Families should plan about 4–8 weeks in India. The same guide lists USA figures from USD 300,000 and a Thailand band of USD 25,000–40,000. Paying a donor is illegal. TechDr will not arrange it.

</section>

## How much does a kidney transplant cost in India?

The band is the recipient plan before donor-evaluation extras, blood-group incompatible treatment, and a long supply of anti-rejection tablets are counted. Donor surgery belongs on its own line. Lifelong tablets are your plan at home unless a short supply is written into the quote. Dialysis, if the operation is delayed, is often excluded.

## Who can donate, and what will the committee check?

A parent, sibling, child, or spouse is the usual legal path under India’s transplant law. Documents proving the relationship are checked, and an authorisation committee can say no. Some unrelated donors are reviewed more strictly and many are refused. A friend cannot sell a kidney. A broker story is a reason to stop the file, not a shortcut.

Do not buy flights before the legal file is underway. If the committee refuses, the surgery does not happen. TechDr cannot overrule that decision.

## How long do the recipient and the donor stay?

Plan several weeks, often 4–8, so blood tests and the donor’s recovery can be watched. A 10-day trip is not realistic. The donor has had an operation too and needs clearance before flying. Heavy work for the recipient often waits 6–12 weeks. Graft survival depends on matching, donor type, and other illnesses. A centre’s audited one-year figures are the numbers worth asking for. A national average is not a personal guarantee.

## What is outside the package?

Paid or falsely described donation, months of immunosuppression, treatment of unrelated heart disease, and a second transplant. High antibody levels, hepatitis, and a previous transplant change the medicines and the price. ABO-incompatible or paired-exchange programmes, where a centre offers them, are add-on plans.

## What should you send?

Relationship proof, dialysis records, viral serology, HLA typing if you already have it, and identification for the donor. Both people need passports. Do not stop dialysis to “save a slot”. Carry a plan for who prescribes the tablets after you land, and a budget for those tablets, before you travel.

## How should you read the quote?

Look for a split between recipient and donor costs, the ward and any ICU cap, and what happens to a deposit if the committee says no. Pay the hospital, not a personal account. Read the [liver donor rules](/blog/liver-transplant-donor-rules) as well. The legal idea is the same even though the operation is different.
