<section id="quick-answer" aria-label="Quick answer">

Take a companion for heart surgery, brain surgery, transplant, major joint replacement, and any plan that includes intensive care or a long flight home. A companion helps with consent if you are drowsy, with medicines, and with the walk through an airport. Hair transplant, cataract, and straightforward LASIK often need an escort on the day rather than a carer for three weeks. The attendant visa is a separate application. It is not granted because a hospital invitation exists.

</section>

## Which trips should not be done alone?

Bypass, valve surgery, craniotomy, kidney or liver transplant, bone marrow transplant, and spinal fusion. You may be in ICU, you may not be allowed to consent, and you should not lift a bag or manage a new medicine list alone. Bariatric patients need someone who understands the liquid diet in the first days. Children need a parent or legal guardian, not a family friend who cannot consent.

A short dental or cataract plan can be reasonable with a local escort for the procedure day and a companion only for the flight if your vision or bite will be unreliable. Ask the team. Do not copy a neighbour’s trip.

## What does a companion actually do?

They hear the consent conversation, keep the medicine chart, notice a fever or a confused night, and speak to the ward if you cannot. They are not a substitute for the nurse, and they are not a clinician. They need their own bed. A companion’s food and hotel are usually outside the surgery package. Write those costs into your budget. A companion who must fly home for work on day three is not a companion for a three-week stay.

## How does the attendant visa fit?

India’s e-Medical Visa and the attendant route are explained in the [visa guide](/blog/india-e-medical-visa-guide). Rules, fees, and eligible nationalities change. Confirm them on the official Indian visa portal. An invitation letter supports an application. It does not issue the visa. Build medical dates after you know both people’s visa windows. Two people refused is a cancelled admission, not a hospital error.

## Who is a poor choice of companion?

Someone who is themselves too unwell to fly, someone who expects to sightsee for the entire stay, and a paid stranger presented as a relative in a transplant file. Relationship documents for living donation are legal documents. An invented relationship is a reason the committee will refuse, and a reason TechDr will stop. Read the [kidney transplant guide](/blog/kidney-transplant-cost-india) before anyone books two tickets for a transplant.

## What should you agree before you fly?

Who holds passports and the hospital letter. Who can approve an extra night of cost if you are asleep. Who speaks to children or an employer at home. The companion’s own medicines. A plan if the companion gets food poisoning or flu, because then you need a backup contact at the hospital. Coordinators can help with logistics. They cannot sit at the bedside all night.
