## How much does a knee replacement cost in India in 2026?

A single primary knee replacement is listed at USD 4,500–7,000. A hip replacement, for comparison, is listed at USD 5,500–8,000. The same guide puts a single knee at USD 35,000–50,000 in the USA and USD 18,000–25,000 in the UK. A chart figure for Thailand is USD 9,000–14,000. All of these are planning ranges. Your written quote can sit outside them if the implant, the hospital, or your health is different.

The useful number is not the lowest number on a website. It is the number on a letter that names the implant, the number of nights, and what happens if you need an extra night in intensive care. TechDr asks partner hospitals for that letter before you pay a deposit or book a non-refundable flight.

## What does the India range usually include?

A standard package in this range is built for one primary knee in a person who is fit for surgery. It usually covers the surgeon and anaesthesia team, the operating theatre, a standard total-knee implant, a ward bed for the planned stay, routine blood tests, routine medicines in hospital, and the first physiotherapy sessions on the ward. Some quotes add airport pickup and a hotel for the companion. Those lines should be written, not assumed.

Ask whether the implant is a named brand or a category such as “imported” or “standard”. Ask whether the patella is resurfaced and whether that is a separate charge. Ask whether the quoted nights are a cap. A five-night quote that bills night six at a daily rate is still an honest quote if the daily rate is printed. It is a poor quote if the daily rate appears only after you arrive.

## What is usually excluded?

The flight, the visa fee, travel insurance, and a companion’s food are outside the surgery line. So is a revision of an old replacement, treatment of an infection, a custom implant, a hinge implant for a very unstable knee, and care for a heart or kidney problem that shows up in the pre-operative tests. Blood transfusion, extra imaging, and a longer stay for a fever are often excluded or capped. The discharge medicines you take home may be included for a few days only.

Physiotherapy after you leave the hospital is the item people forget. A knee that is not bent and straightened on a schedule gets stiff. If the quote stops at the hospital door, budget for sessions in India and a written exercise sheet for the physiotherapist at home.

## How long do you stay in hospital and in India?

An uncomplicated primary knee often means 5–7 nights in hospital. The total time in India is commonly 2–3 weeks so the wound can be checked, the swelling can settle, and a clinician can say whether a long flight is reasonable. That second opinion on the flight is medical, not a travel-agent preference. A swollen calf, a fever, a draining wound, or pain that is out of proportion to the day after surgery is a reason to stay, not a reason to catch the ticket you already bought.

Bilateral replacements, meaning both knees in one admission, are not a simple doubling of the single-knee stay. Blood loss, pain, and the risk of a clot are higher. Some people are safer with one knee now and the second knee on a later trip. The quote should say which plan the surgeon recommends and should price each implant.

## How does the price compare with treatment at home?

Using the midpoints of the 2026 guide, a single knee in India sits below the USA and UK ranges printed on the cost page. After flights and a hotel, many people still spend less than a cash price at home, but the honest comparison includes time off work for you and for the person who travels with you. A cheap surgery plus a cancelled flight home because of a clot is not a saving.

Do not compare an Indian “package” with a home-country “surgeon fee” alone. At home the hospital, the implant, the anaesthesia, and the ward are often billed separately. In a medical-travel quote they are often bundled. Put both lists side by side. If one list has the implant and the other does not, you are not comparing the same operation.

## Which implant details change the quote?

A standard cemented or uncemented total knee from a widely used manufacturer is what the USD 4,500–7,000 band assumes. A different bearing surface, a patient-specific cutting guide, a robot-assisted plan, or a revision system for a failed old knee can move the price. Robot assistance is a method, not a promise of a better knee. If it is offered, the quote should say what it adds in money and what it does not change about recovery.

Partial replacement, which resurfaces only the worn compartment, can be appropriate for a smaller group of people. It is not a discount version of a total knee. If your X-rays show wear in more than one compartment, a partial knee can fail early and you may need a total knee later. The surgeon should say why one option fits your films.

## Who is a reasonable candidate to travel?

People who travel for a knee usually have pain and stiffness that limit walking after a proper trial of physiotherapy, weight management, and medicines, and an X-ray that matches that story. An X-ray alone is not a reason to fly. Neither is a brochure. If you still walk comfortably on pain relief, a local clinician may reasonably advise waiting.

People who need extra planning include those with a body mass index in a high range, diabetes that is not stable, a recent heart event, a previous clot, or an infection anywhere in the body. None of these is an automatic refusal. They are reasons for blood tests, a physician review, and sometimes a delay. TechDr will not book a flight while an infection is untreated.

## What should you send before a quote?

Send a short history, a list of medicines, allergies, standing knee X-rays that are recent, and any MRI if you have one. Add blood tests if you have them, especially haemoglobin, kidney function, and HbA1c if you have diabetes. Name the country you will fly from and whether someone will travel with you. A quote that ignores your medicines is incomplete, because blood thinners and some diabetes drugs change the day of surgery.

Photos of the knee are less useful than films. If you can only send a photo of an X-ray on a screen, say so, and expect the hospital to ask for the actual file before a final plan.

## How should you read the written quote?

Check five lines. First, one knee or two. Second, the implant name or an honest statement that the brand will be confirmed before admission. Third, the number of ward nights and the price of an extra night. Fourth, whether ICU is included and for how long. Fifth, the exclusions, in a list, not in a slogan. Then check who you pay. Paying a hospital invoice in the hospital’s name is clearer than paying an individual to “hold a bed”.

A deposit before the visa is sometimes requested. It should be written, refundable if the visa is refused or if the surgeon later says you are not fit, and small relative to the total. TechDr does not ask you to pay a stranger’s personal account.

## What are the risks you should plan around?

Bleeding, infection, stiffness, a clot in the leg or lung, nerve or vessel injury, and a knee that is not perfectly straight are known risks of replacement. Reported series from high-volume units often describe good pain relief for suitable patients. That is a reported range, not a guarantee for you. Smoking, poorly controlled diabetes, and operating through an infection raise the chance of a wound problem. The hospital should tell you which risks apply to your file, in writing, before you consent.

Pain after a knee replacement is expected. Uncontrolled pain, a sudden calf swelling, breathlessness, or a fever is not something to watch overnight in a hotel. Those symptoms need the hospital that operated, or the nearest emergency department if you have already flown home.

## How do the visa and the city fit the date?

The India e-Medical Visa is a separate process from the surgery quote. Rules change. Confirm the current form, fee, and eligible nationalities on the official Indian visa portal before you pay for tickets. An invitation letter from a hospital supports an application. It is not the visa. Build the medical dates after you know the visa window, not the other way around.

Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Kolkata can all host an accredited orthopaedic admission. Choose the city that matches the surgeon’s plan and a hotel close enough for wound checks. A “right city” list is less useful than a direct flight and a companion who can stay the full two to three weeks.

## What should you arrange before you fly home?

Ask for the operation note, the implant stickers, the medicine list, and the exercise sheet. Carry them in your cabin bag. A policy that excludes planned surgery abroad will not pay if the wound opens. If you are cleared around two to three weeks after an uncomplicated knee, you still need to walk on the flight. Do not fly because the fare is cheaper that day.
