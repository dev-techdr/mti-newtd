<section id="quick-answer" aria-label="Quick answer">

Send a short history, a medicine and allergy list, the actual scan files, recent blood tests, and the country you will fly from. A written quote from a TechDr partner hospital follows that file. A WhatsApp photo of a report can start a conversation. It is not enough for a final plan, a deposit, or a flight.

</section>

## Which documents belong in every file?

A one-page history in your own words: what hurts or what was diagnosed, what has already been tried, and what you want to know. The medicine list with doses, including blood thinners, diabetes drugs, steroids, and prostate drugs. Allergies. Previous operations. Whether someone will travel with you. Your age and the passport country, because visa rules and some treatment rules depend on them.

Do not send a request that says only “best hospital, cheapest price”. That sentence cannot be priced.

## Which scans and tests matter by specialty?

Joints need recent X-rays, and an MRI if you have one. Spine and brain opinions need the image files, not only the radiologist’s paragraph. Heart surgery needs the angiogram and the echo. Transplant files need donor identity and relationship proof as well as medical tests. Cancer files need the pathology report, and slides if the team asks to confirm the diagnosis. Dental and hair plans need the relevant images or unedited photos. If you can only photograph a screen, say so, and expect a request for the real file before a number is locked.

## What should you leave out?

Other people’s reports mixed into the same album. Edited hair photos. A price you were promised by someone who has not seen the file. Do not send card numbers, internet-banking passwords, or copies of a card front and back to a chat account. Identity documents for a visa are a separate, later step. The [visa guide](/blog/india-e-medical-visa-guide) explains the invitation letter. The letter is not the visa.

## How fast is an opinion, and what does it not decide?

A remote review can prevent a wasted flight. It cannot manage chest pain tonight, a falling conscious level, or a fever after surgery you already had. Those need local emergency care. Read [how a second opinion works](/blog/free-second-opinion-online). An opinion also does not book the operating list. The hospital admits. A case manager coordinates.

## How should you name the files?

Use your name and the test type. “MRI” as a filename on a compressed folder of twelve mixed studies slows the review. Say the date of each study. If a report is not in English, send it anyway and say which language it is. A translation of the conclusion helps. The images still matter more than the translation.

## What happens after the file is enough?

You should receive a letter that names the operation, what is included, what is excluded, the nights, and what an extra night costs. If those lines are missing, you do not have a quote yet. The [package guide](/blog/whats-included-in-a-medical-tourism-package) is the checklist. Pay a hospital invoice when you pay.
